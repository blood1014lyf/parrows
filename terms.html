2. Scope of Services

Hammer & Wedge provides high-end spatial transformation services divided across three principal disciplines:

  • Spaces (Interior Architecture): Spatial zoning, schematic layouts, technical drawings, mechanical/electrical/plumbing (MEP) schematics, and 3D architectural visualizations.
  • Renovations (Turnkey Transformations): Demolition oversight, structural remodeling, screeding, tiling, architectural ceilings, sanitary ware installation, and general building refurbishment.
  • Furnishing (Bespoke Joinery & Curation): Custom manufacture of walnut cabinetry, dressing rooms, upholstered suites, imported designer fixtures, art consultation, and styling.

Clients acknowledge and accept that natural materials possess inherent variations in grain structure, color tone, veining, patina development, and texture. Such natural deviations do not constitute defects; rather, they are the hallmarks of authentic luxury and handcrafted artisanal excellence.
