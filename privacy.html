Privacy Policy

How Hammer & Wedge collects, uses, protects, and handles personal data in adherence with the Ghana Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843) and international data governance frameworks.

1. Introduction & Commitment

Hammer & Wedge ("we", "us", or "our") operates a boutique interior architecture, turnkey renovation, and luxury furnishing studio based in Airport Residential Area, Accra, Ghana. We are firmly committed to safeguarding the privacy, confidentiality, and integrity of the personal data entrusted to us by our prospective and existing clients, partners, and site visitors.

This Privacy Policy delineates our data collection, handling, and protection practices in compliance with the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) of the Republic of Ghana, the guidelines issued by the Data Protection Commission (DPC) of Ghana, and international standards including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for our international and diaspora clientele.

2. Data Controller Details

For the purposes of applicable data protection legislation, the Data Controller responsible for your personal data is:

  • Legal Entity: Hammer & Wedge
  • Physical Address: 14 Senchi Street, Airport Residential Area, Accra, Greater Accra Region, Ghana
  • Telephone: +233 55 123 4567
  • Privacy & Data Protection Desk: privacy@hammerandwedge.com

3. Information We Collect

We collect personal information directly when you initiate contact with us via our website enquiry forms, direct correspondence, telephone, or during scheduled private studio consultations:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full name, telephone number, WhatsApp contact details, email address.
  • Project & Property Information: Project location/address, property type (private residence, penthouse, corporate suite, boutique commercial), architectural scope, timeline expectations, and design preferences.
  • Communication Records: Records of design inquiries, consultation notes, agreed specifications, and feedback.
  • Technical Data & Telemetry: Anonymized or pseudonymous technical telemetry collected automatically upon visiting our site (e.g. IP address, browser type, device characteristics, operating system, and session timestamps) used strictly for service optimization and security diagnostics.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

In accordance with Section 20 of the Ghana Data Protection Act (Act 843), we only process your personal data under valid lawful grounds:

Purpose of Processing Lawful Basis under Act 843 / GDPR
Responding to client project enquiries and scheduling consultations Consent (Section 20(1) Act 843) & Pre-contractual steps (Section 20(2)(a))
Preparing architectural proposals, quotations, and bills of quantities Performance of contract or preliminary steps prior to entering contract
Managing project delivery, on-site renovation coordination, and furnishing delivery Performance of contract (Section 20(2)(a) Act 843)
Accounting, invoicing, and tax statutory reporting in Ghana Compliance with statutory legal obligations (Section 20(2)(b) Act 843)
Website security, DDoS mitigation, and operational analytics Legitimate business interests (Section 20(2)(e) Act 843)

5. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information solely for legitimate business operations:

  • To review your interior design, turnkey renovation, or custom joinery requirements and formulate tailored design concepts.
  • To communicate with you regarding project milestones, material approvals, site inspections, and handover schedules.
  • To maintain internal records, contracts, warranties, and client care records.
  • We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information to any third parties or marketing brokers.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses minimal, privacy-conscious technologies:

  • Essential Cookies / Storage: Required for the fundamental technical operation of the website, including keeping track of your cookie consent preferences and session security.
  • Preference Storage: Remembers your display preferences and accessibility settings (such as prefers-reduced-motion preferences).
  • You can inspect, manage, or clear cookies through your browser settings or via our in-site Cookie Preferences panel at any time.

7. Data Retention & Disposal

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was gathered:

  • General Enquiries: Retained for twenty-four (24) months following the last communication, after which records are purged unless an active engagement ensues.
  • Contracted Projects: Retained for seven (7) years following project completion to honor architectural warranties, statutory accounting guidelines, and tax regulations in Ghana.
  • Upon expiration of the retention window, physical documents are securely shredded and digital files are permanently erased.

8. Data Security & Confidentiality

We implement stringent physical, technical, and organizational security controls to prevent unauthorized access, accidental alteration, disclosure, or destruction of personal data:

  • All data transmissions across our website are secured via Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Access to client information is restricted exclusively to authorized design team leads and project supervisors on a strict need-to-know basis.
  • Digital assets, design drawings, and architectural briefs are stored within access-controlled, encrypted repositories.

9. Your Statutory Rights

Under the Ghana Data Protection Act 2012 and applicable international privacy laws, you possess the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You may request confirmation and copies of your personal data held by us.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or out-of-date information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the deletion of your personal records where there is no statutory legal imperative for us to retain them.
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: You have the right to object to or restrict processing of your data on legitimate grounds.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is predicated on consent, you may revoke your consent at any time without retroactive detriment.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please submit a written request to our Privacy Desk at privacy@hammerandwedge.com. We respond to all verified requests within twenty-one (21) business days free of charge.
