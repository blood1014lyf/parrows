Architectural spaces and custom furniture crafted in Accra.

Privacy Policy

1. Statutory Governance & Data Controller

Parrow’s Signature operates under the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and follows international data governance principles (GDPR). Our registered studio is at 14 Senchi Street, Airport Residential Area, Accra, Ghana. Official contact: privacy@parrowssignature.com.

2. Data Collection & Lawful Basis

We collect identity and project information (names, emails, WhatsApp contacts, project specifications) strictly with your consent under Section 20 of Act 843 to assess design feasibility and execute commissions.

3. Non-Disclosure & Security

We never sell, monetize, or disclose your personal information to third-party advertisers. All project data is transmitted over encrypted TLS connections and maintained in restricted-access studio repositories.

4. Your Statutory Rights

You retain full rights to request access, rectification, erasure, or restriction of your personal data at any time. Inquiries may be addressed to our Data Protection Desk, or to the Data Protection Commission (DPC) of Ghana.
